## Before you leave
- [ ] Fresh batteries in the H5essential, plus spares. A USB power bank is a backup.
- [ ] SD card formatted **in the recorder**, with enough free space (≈1.1 GB per hour stereo).
- [ ] Mic stand, stereo bar, 2 × XLR, sE8 clips and windshields.
- [ ] Check the recorder's date and time (it sets the file timestamps).

## Setup
- [ ] Stand centred and high (see [Mic placement](/logic-choir-workflow/recording/mic-placement/)).
- [ ] Phantom power on. Pad off, HPF on the mics at 80 Hz or off.
- [ ] Short test recording, then listen on headphones to check both channels and that left/right are the right way round.
- [ ] Tape down cables. Ask the venue about anything noisy (heating, fridges, air con).

## Recording
- [ ] Hit record **before** the audience settles and leave it running. Splitting is easy later.
- [ ] Record **30 seconds of room tone** (silence in the room) before or after. It's used for noise reduction and for filling gaps.
- [ ] Clap or slate at the start.
- [ ] Jot down rough times of songs you'll want, plus any incidents (cough at 23:10, door at 41:00).

## After
- [ ] Stop recording and check the file plays back.
- [ ] Copy off the card **the same day**. See [Files and backups](/logic-choir-workflow/ingest/files-and-backups/).
