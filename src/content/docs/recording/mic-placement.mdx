Current setup: **two sE8s on one stand, a few metres from the singers, angled about 90° apart.**
If the capsules nearly touch, that's an **XY** pair. If they're about 17 cm apart, it's closer to **ORTF**. Check this next time, because it changes how the stereo image sounds.

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="XY (coincident)">
    Capsules touching, one above the other, angled **90–135°**.
    - ✅ Mono-compatible and focused, with no phasing problems.
    - ❌ Can sound narrow. At 90° the group bunches into the centre.
    - 💡 Try **110–120°** to widen the image, or move closer.
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="ORTF (near-coincident)">
    Capsules **17 cm apart**, angled **110°** outward.
    - ✅ A wider, more "airy" image than XY, with a natural sense of space. A common choice for choirs.
    - ❌ Slightly less mono-solid, though fine in practice.
    - 💡 Needs a stereo bar (cheap) on the stand.
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Spaced pair (AB)">
    Two mics **60 cm–1 m+ apart**, both facing the group.
    - ✅ Very wide and spacious, with good low end.
    - ❌ Vague centre image and phase issues in mono. Usually needs two stands.
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Distance and height

- **Distance**: the critical distance is where the direct sound equals the room's reverb. Closer gives a drier, clearer sound with less room. For 8 singers, 2–4 m is typical. Adding reverb later is easy, removing it is impossible, so **err slightly close**.
- **Height**: raise the mics above the singers' heads (about 2.5–3 m), aimed down at the middle of the group. This evens out front and back rows and gets above the audience.
- **Centre** the stand on the group, not on the room.

<Aside type="tip">
Clap once in front of the mics at the start. It makes a clean sync point and a quick check on the stereo image (the clap should sit dead centre).
</Aside>

## Planned: shared boom stand (K&M 21021)

At the last concert the mics were mounted at camera height on the camera stand, to keep them out of the shot. That put them too low. The plan is to buy a **K&M 21021** boom stand and use **one stand for both**:

- **iPhone on the upright**, clamped at about **1.6 m** (eye level). This is the centre camera, at the same spot as now (see [Camera placement](/logic-choir-workflow/recording/camera-placement/)).
- **sE8 pair on the boom arm**, on the stereo bar at the far end, with the boom raised and reaching **towards the singers**.

Approximate specs (they vary slightly between retailers, so check the actual stand): upright **about 1.1–2.0 m**, boom **about 1.07 m**.

### Why it works

- **Higher.** With the upright at full height (about 2.0 m) and the boom angled up, the mics end up around **2.5–2.8 m**. That's the height recommended above, so they're above the singers' heads and above the audience.
- **Closer.** The boom puts the mics up to about **0.5–0.9 m** in front of the camera, depending on the angle and how much boom sits behind the clamp. With the camera at about 4.3 m, the mics end up at about **3.5 m**, inside the 2–4 m range.
- **Out of shot.** At eye level and level, the 1x lens sees only about 23° above the horizontal. The boom starts at about 2.0 m, right above the phone, and the mics are high up and in front, so all of it is well above the top of the frame.

### Setting it up

1. Put the stand at the centre camera's marked spot, with **one tripod leg pointing forward** under the boom so it doesn't tip.
2. Set the upright height first, then clamp the phone at about 1.6 m using a phone holder that clamps onto the mic stand.
3. Mount the stereo bar and both sE8s on the end of the boom. Raise the boom and aim the mics down at the middle of the row.
4. Run the XLR cables along the boom and down the upright **behind** the phone, then tape them down at the base.
5. Check the iPhone's shot. No boom, mic, cable or clip should be in frame, including at the top edge.

<Aside type="caution" title="One stand means shared vibration">
Anything that touches the phone (tapping record, adjusting the framing) goes straight up the stand into the mics. **Start the iPhone before the clap and don't touch the stand during the concert.** If knocks still come through, shock mounts for the sE8s will help.
</Aside>
