Current setup: **two sE8s on one stand, a few metres from the singers, angled about 90° apart.**
If the capsules nearly touch, that's an **XY** pair. If they're about 17 cm apart, it's closer to **ORTF**. Check this next time, because it changes how the stereo image sounds.

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="XY (coincident)">
    Capsules touching, one above the other, angled **90–135°**.
    - ✅ Mono-compatible and focused, with no phasing problems.
    - ❌ Can sound narrow. At 90° the group bunches into the centre.
    - 💡 Try **110–120°** to widen the image, or move closer.
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="ORTF (near-coincident)">
    Capsules **17 cm apart**, angled **110°** outward.
    - ✅ A wider, more "airy" image than XY, with a natural sense of space. A common choice for choirs.
    - ❌ Slightly less mono-solid, though fine in practice.
    - 💡 Needs a stereo bar (cheap) on the stand.
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Spaced pair (AB)">
    Two mics **60 cm–1 m+ apart**, both facing the group.
    - ✅ Very wide and spacious, with good low end.
    - ❌ Vague centre image and phase issues in mono. Usually needs two stands.
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Distance and height

- **Distance**: the critical distance is where the direct sound equals the room's reverb. Closer gives a drier, clearer sound with less room. For 8 singers, 2–4 m is typical. Adding reverb later is easy, removing it is impossible, so **err slightly close**.
- **Height**: raise the mics above the singers' heads (about 2.5–3 m), aimed down at the middle of the group. This evens out front and back rows and gets above the audience.
- **Centre** the stand on the group, not on the room.

<Aside type="tip">
Clap once in front of the mics at the start. It makes a clean sync point and a quick check on the stereo image (the clap should sit dead centre).
</Aside>
