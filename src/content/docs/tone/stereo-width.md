---
title: Stereo width
description: Checking and adjusting the stereo image.
sidebar:
  order: 3
---

- **Check mono**: put Logic's **Direction Mixer** or **Gain** plugin (Mono button) on the stereo output, then flick to mono. If the sound gets hollow or thin, there are phase problems (unlikely with XY).
- **Widen** a narrow XY recording: Direction Mixer **Spread** slightly above 1.0, or a mid/side EQ that lifts the sides a little in the 2–8 kHz range. Be subtle, because too much sounds phasey.
- **Balance**: if one side of the group is louder (mics off-centre), use Direction Mixer **Direction** or the *Balance* knob to re-centre the soloists or the group.
- **Correlation meter**: Logic's *Correlation Meter* plugin should stay mostly between 0 and +1.
