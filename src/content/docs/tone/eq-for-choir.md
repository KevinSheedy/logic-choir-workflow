Small moves only: **±1–3 dB**, wide bands. You're shaping eight voices and a room at once.

| Range | What lives there | Typical move |
|---|---|---|
| < 70 Hz | Rumble, not voice | High-pass (see [Rumble](/logic-choir-workflow/cleanup/rumble-and-noise/)) |
| 100–250 Hz | Bass/baritone body, room boom | Cut 1–3 dB if boomy or muddy |
| 250–500 Hz | "Boxiness", mud | Gentle wide cut if it sounds congested |
| 2–5 kHz | Presence, consonants, clarity | Small boost if distant. Cut if harsh (sopranos) |
| 8–12 kHz | Air, sibilance | Gentle high shelf for sparkle. Watch the "s" sounds |

## Method
1. Loop the loudest passage.
2. Sweep a narrow boost to *find* a problem, then turn it into a wide, gentle cut.
3. Bypass often (compare at matched loudness, since louder always sounds better).
4. Reference a commercial a cappella recording (e.g. VOCES8, The King's Singers) at a matched level.
