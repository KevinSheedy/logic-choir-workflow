Live recordings always have some audience noise. Fix the ones that **distract on a second listen** and leave the rest.

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Volume dip">
    Draw a short volume-automation dip (−3 to −10 dB) over the cough.
    - ✅ Quick, built into Logic, and enough for most coughs during loud passages.
    - ❌ Audible in quiet passages because the singing dips too.
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Edit & crossfade">
    If a phrase repeats (e.g. a verse or chorus), or the noise falls in a pause, **copy clean audio** over it with a crossfade. Room tone can fill pauses.
    - ✅ Free and invisible when it works.
    - ❌ Only works when matching material exists.
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Spectral repair">
    Paint the cough out of a spectrogram, leaving the voices.
    - **iZotope RX** (Elements / Standard): the industry standard. *Spectral De-noise*, *Mouth De-click* and *Spectral Repair* (Standard).
    - **Steinberg SpectraLayers**: a strong alternative with similar spectral editing.
    - **Acon Digital Restoration Suite**: cheaper, with De-click and De-noise.
    - ✅ Near-magic on coughs over sustained chords.
    - ❌ Costs money and has a learning curve. Use it as an external editor alongside Logic.
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>
