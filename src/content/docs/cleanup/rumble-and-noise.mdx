## Rumble (do this always)
Low-frequency rumble from traffic, HVAC, footsteps and handling eats headroom and muddies the sound.

- Add **Channel EQ** with the **high-pass filter** on, at **60–80 Hz**, slope 12–24 dB/oct.
- Listen to the basses' lowest notes. Raise the frequency until they start to thin, then back off.
- If the sE8s' 80 Hz HPF was on at the gig, you may need little or none.

## Steady noise (hiss, air con, fridge hum)

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Leave it">
    Small amounts of room noise read as "live" and get masked by singing and reverb. **Try this first.**
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Gentle broadband NR">
    Use a noise-reduction plugin that learns a noise profile from **room tone** (another reason to record it).
    - Free or cheap: Logic has no dedicated NR. Options include **Acon Digital DeNoise**, **Waves NS1/Clarity**, and **iZotope RX Elements**.
    - Be gentle: 3–6 dB reduction. Too much causes "underwater" artefacts on the reverb tail.
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Hum notch">
    Mains hum at **50 Hz** (UK/EU) plus harmonics: narrow Channel EQ notches at 50, 100 and 150 Hz. Only do this if you can hear it.
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

<Aside type="tip">Noise reduction always goes **before** reverb and compression in the chain.</Aside>
