## The pipeline

<Steps>
1. **Record**: a stereo pair of sE8s into the H5essential, 32-bit float. [Recording →](recording/gear/)
2. **Ingest**: back up, rename, and import into a Logic project. [Ingest →](ingest/files-and-backups/)
3. **Select & cut**: mark each song, then cut it out with handles. [Editing →](editing/finding-songs/)
4. **Clean up**: remove rumble, coughs, clicks and noise. [Clean-up →](cleanup/rumble-and-noise/)
5. **Tone & balance**: EQ, gentle dynamics, stereo image. [Tone →](tone/eq-for-choir/)
6. **Space**: add reverb that flatters the voices without washing them out. [Reverb →](reverb/why-and-how-much/)
7. **Loudness & export**: set the final level, then bounce masters and share copies. [Export →](export/loudness/)
8. **Log it**: write down what worked in the [concert log](concerts/template/) and the [changelog](reference/changelog/).
</Steps>
