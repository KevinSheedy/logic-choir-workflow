Set a **cycle region** over the song (including the fade), then use *File → Bounce → Project or Section* (`Cmd`‑`B`).

## Master (archive)
- **PCM (WAV)**, **24-bit**, project sample rate (48 kHz), *Dithering*: **POW-r #1** (or none for 32-bit float).
- Mode: **Offline** (fast), with *Normalize* **off**, since the limiter handles level.

## Share copies
- **MP3** 320 kbps or **AAC** 256 kbps, made from the master in the same bounce dialog (tick multiple formats).
- YouTube: upload the 24-bit WAV (or a high-bitrate AAC) with a still image or video.

## Naming
`YYYY-MM-DD_Ascolta_SongTitle_v1.wav`. Bump `v2`, `v3` when you remix rather than overwriting.

## Final checks
- [ ] Listen to the whole bounce on headphones, then on a phone speaker, and in the car if possible.
- [ ] No clicks at the start or end, and the fade sounds complete.
- [ ] Add the song to the concert log.
