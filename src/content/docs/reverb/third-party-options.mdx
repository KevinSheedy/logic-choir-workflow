<Aside type="caution">Prices are approximate (USD, checked Oct 2026) and change often. Big sales (Black Friday, Dec–Jan) can cut 25–50%. **Always use the demo first.**</Aside>

| Plugin | Type | ~Price | Character | Why for choir | Trial |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Valhalla Supermassive** | Algorithmic | Free | Huge, lush, ambient | Free to try, but mostly *too* big. Good for learning parameters | n/a |
| **Valhalla VintageVerb** | Algorithmic | $50 | Classic 70s/80s hall & plate | Superb value with smooth halls. A very common recommendation | Unlimited, with noise |
| **Valhalla Room** | Algorithmic | $50 | Natural rooms and halls | Realistic, transparent, easy to blend with a real room | Unlimited, with noise |
| **FabFilter Pro-R 2** | Algorithmic | ~$199 | Clean, natural, modern | Excellent UI, decay EQ, and *Space* control that's easy to dial in | 30-day full |
| **LiquidSonics Cinematic Rooms** | Algorithmic | ~$199 (Pro) | Very smooth, high-end rooms & halls | Top-tier realism for acoustic music | 14-day |
| **LiquidSonics Seventh Heaven** | Convolution-style | Pro ~$299 (cheaper edition exists) | Bricasti M7 sound: *the* classical hall reverb | Bricasti halls are a classical/choral industry standard | 14-day |
| **Relab LX480** | Algorithmic | ~$150–250 | Lexicon 480L recreation | Iconic, lush hall sound | Demo |
| **Audio Ease Altiverb** | Convolution | ~$500+ | Real cathedrals and concert halls | Huge library of real spaces. Pricey | Contact vendor |

## Shortlist (suggested)
1. **Valhalla VintageVerb or Room** ($50): a big upgrade for very little money.
2. **FabFilter Pro-R 2**: if you value a fast, clear UI and plan to buy more FabFilter plugins.
3. **LiquidSonics Seventh Heaven**: if you want *the* classical hall sound and can afford it.

Run each through the [evaluation protocol](/logic-choir-workflow/reverb/evaluation-protocol/) against ChromaVerb and Space Designer before buying.
