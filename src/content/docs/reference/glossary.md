- **32-bit float**: an audio format with effectively unlimited headroom. It can't clip at the recorder.
- **Aux / Bus**: a channel that receives audio sent from other tracks. Used for shared effects like reverb.
- **dBFS**: decibels relative to digital full scale. 0 dBFS is the fixed-point maximum.
- **dBTP**: true-peak level, including peaks between samples.
- **Dither**: low-level noise added when reducing bit depth, to avoid distortion.
- **HPF (high-pass filter)**: removes frequencies *below* a cutoff.
- **IR (impulse response)**: a recorded "fingerprint" of a space, used by convolution reverb.
- **LUFS**: loudness units relative to full scale. Perceived loudness over time.
- **Mid/Side (M/S)**: splitting stereo into the centre (mid) and the difference (side) for separate processing.
- **ORTF / XY / AB**: stereo mic techniques. See [Mic placement](/logic-choir-workflow/recording/mic-placement/).
- **Pre-delay**: the gap before a reverb starts. It keeps the original sound clear.
- **RT60 / Decay**: the time for a reverb to fall by 60 dB.
- **Room tone**: a recording of the room's "silence", used to fill gaps and profile noise.
